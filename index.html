The Destination
CHIANG MAI &
NORTHERN THAILAND

One of Southeast Asia’s most spectacular and underexplored regions. Ancient temples, misty mountain peaks, jungle trails — all centred around Chiang Mai, the cultural capital of the north. And extraordinary from the air.

Mountain Landscapes
Doi Inthanon — Thailand’s highest peak at 2,565m — and dramatic highland scenery unlike anywhere else in Southeast Asia. Breathtaking from the air.
Legendary Roads
The Mae Hong Son Loop — 600km of winding mountain passes, jungle valleys and highland villages. One of the great motorcycle journeys on earth.
Ancient Lanna Culture
Over 300 temples, a 700-year-old walled city and the living traditions of the Lanna Kingdom make Chiang Mai one of Asia’s richest cultural destinations.
Wild, Untouched Nature
Dense jungle national parks, dramatic waterfalls, terraced rice fields and remote hill-tribe villages. Northern Thailand’s landscapes are endlessly diverse.
World-Class Sunsets
The golden hour over Chiang Mai’s valley is extraordinary. Watching the sun set over Doi Suthep from 1,000 ft in a gyrocopter — one of the most beautiful things you’ll ever see.
Northern Thai Cuisine
Khao Soi, Sai Oua, Larb — Northern Thai cuisine is distinct from the south and considered by many food lovers to be Thailand’s finest. World-class and deeply authentic.

The perfect base for adventure. World-class infrastructure, incredible nature, and a completely different world from Bangkok. 700km north and a million miles away.
